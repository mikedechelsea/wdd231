About Me

Hello! I'm Mike Asiabaka, a Software Development student at BYU-Pathway, originally from Ile-Ife, Nigeria. I have over five years of experience in data analytics, helping organizations turn raw data into meaningful insights that support better decision-making.

I'm passionate about bridging the gap between software development and data analytics. My experience spans SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, and Google Looker Studio, with a focus on building data pipelines, automating workflows, and creating interactive dashboards that make complex information easier to understand.

Beyond data analytics, I'm expanding my skills in web development using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I enjoy solving problems, exploring new technologies, and building practical solutions that make work more efficient.

When I'm not working with data or coding, I enjoy spending time with my family, learning new things, and serving in my community. My goal is to combine software development and data analytics to build innovative, accessible, and impactful solutions.
